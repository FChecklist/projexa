Add PROJEXA to your AI

Use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini to work on your PROJEXA projects. You do it once. After that you just talk to your AI.

Step 1: copy this address

https://pcrjmlpuqsbocqfwoxod.supabase.co/functions/v1/projexa-oauth/mcp

Step 2: paste it into your AI

Claude

  1. Open Claude and go to Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Click Add custom connector.
  3. Type the name PROJEXA and paste the address from Step 1. Click Add.
  4. Click Connect. A PROJEXA page opens. Sign in if asked, then click Allow.

ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT, then Settings, then Apps (or Connectors).
  2. Turn on Developer mode if it asks (some plans need it for your own connector).
  3. Click Create or Add, name it PROJEXA, and paste the address from Step 1.
  4. Choose OAuth / sign in when asked. A PROJEXA page opens. Click Allow.

Gemini

  1. Open Gemini, then Settings, then Connected apps (or your company's Gemini admin page).
  2. Add a custom MCP server and paste the address from Step 1.
  3. When it asks you to sign in, a PROJEXA page opens. Click Allow.

Menu names change often. If you do not see one of these words, look for "connector", "app" or "MCP". Your company's admin may have to switch it on first.

What your AI can do

  • Read your projects and records, and draft changes. You confirm every change inside PROJEXA.
  • It sees only what your own role allows, never more.
  • The connection lasts 30 days. Then your AI will ask you to Allow again.

Try it

Ask your AI: "Using PROJEXA, list my projects and tell me what needs my attention."

Switch it off

Open PROJEXA, go to your AI link settings, and revoke the link named after your AI. It stops working at once.
