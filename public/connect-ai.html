Use your AI with PROJEXA

Let Claude or ChatGPT read your projects, build reports and prepare changes for you. You set it up once. After that you just talk to your AI.

Claude recommendedAdd PROJEXA once. Claude then works on its own: it looks things up, runs reports and can make changes for you. Works on the free plan. ChatGPTNothing to install: copy one prompt from PROJEXA and paste it. ChatGPT reads your projects and answers; for any change it gives you a link to tap.

Either way: your AI sees only what your own role allows, never more. You can switch it off at any time (see the end of each part).

A. Claude (recommended)

You add PROJEXA to Claude as a connector. This takes about two minutes. You need a Claude account (free is enough) and your PROJEXA login.

Step 1. Copy this address

https://pcrjmlpuqsbocqfwoxod.supabase.co/functions/v1/projexa-oauth/mcp

Step 2. Add it in Claude

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. Click your name or initials, then Settings.
  2. Open Connectors.
  3. Click Add custom connector. Type the name PROJEXA, paste the address from Step 1, and click Add.
  4. Click Connect next to PROJEXA. A PROJEXA page opens.
  5. On that page, sign in to PROJEXA if it asks. You will then see: "Let Claude use PROJEXA as you?"
  6. Click Allow. (Leave the small box ticked off unless you want Claude to make changes without asking you each time.) You are sent back to Claude and PROJEXA shows as connected.
Using Claude for a team (Team or Enterprise plan)? The Claude owner adds the connector once in Admin settings, Connectors. Then each person opens Settings, Connectors, and clicks Connect next to PROJEXA, and clicks Allow.

Step 3. Use it

  1. Start a new chat. Next to the message box, open the tools or + menu and make sure PROJEXA is switched on.
  2. Type: Using PROJEXA, list my projects and tell me what needs my attention.
  3. Claude may ask permission before it uses a tool. Choose Allow. For reading, Always allow is fine. Keep asking for anything that changes or removes data.
A good first answer shows your projects as a numbered list, then a menu of areas (where things stand, scope of work and BOQ, work progress, budget, billing, change orders, manpower, schedule, timesheets, documents, projects). Every answer ends with three lines: DONE (what it read or did), NEXT (your options) and ASK (what it needs from you).

Switch it off

In Claude: Settings, Connectors, PROJEXA, then Remove (or Disconnect). In PROJEXA: open your AI link settings and revoke the link named Claude (sign-in). It stops working at once.

B. ChatGPT

This uses one prompt that you copy from PROJEXA. There is nothing to install. ChatGPT reads your projects from the page that prompt points to. It can answer questions and build reports. For a change, it gives you a link and you tap Confirm.

  1. Sign in to PROJEXA (projexa-ai.com). Click the Connect button at the top and then Prompt copied. The whole message is now on your clipboard.
  2. Open chatgpt.com and start a new chat. Switch Search on (in the tools or + menu next to the message box). ChatGPT only opens web addresses when Search is on.
  3. Paste the message and press Enter. Do not add anything else and do not paste anything again later.
  4. ChatGPT shows your projects as a numbered list. Type a project number, then ask for what you need.
A good first answer shows your projects numbered, a menu of areas, and ends with DONE, NEXT and ASK. Ask next, for example: Show me the Scope of work and BOQ of Project 15. or Check this project for problems.

Making a change with ChatGPT

  1. Ask in plain words, for example Record 10% progress on item EX-01.
  2. ChatGPT shows you what it will do and gives you one link on its own line.
  3. Click the link. A PROJEXA page shows the change in plain words (and, for a deletion, exactly what will be removed). Tap Confirm.
  4. Tell ChatGPT it is done, and it checks.
Treat the message you copied like a password: it lets an AI act as you, within your role. Use it only in your own chat, never in a shared chat or team workspace.
Something is not working

ChatGPT says it cannot open the address. Check that Search is on and that you started a new chat. Paste the message again as the first message. The address must be at the very end.

It lists projects but cannot show details. This used to happen when it tried to open a second address. If it happens, say: Everything is on the page I gave you. Read it again and answer from it.

It says the link has expired. Links last a few days. Go back to PROJEXA, press Connect and Prompt copied again, and start a new chat.

I want it to make changes without tapping each time. Use Claude with the connector (part A) and tick the box on the Allow page. ChatGPT's free and Plus plans cannot add a connector.

Switch it off

Open your AI link settings in PROJEXA and revoke the link. The pasted message stops working at once.
